DomLux

Premium brand front-end – Pixel Perfect, aesthetics, performance.

Brief

A website for a luxury furniture manufacturer required a precise reproduction of the graphic design with a strong emphasis on aesthetics, minimalism and visual details that reflect the premium nature of the brand.

Maintaining high visual quality, smooth transitions and full responsiveness were critical – especially for photo presentation and layout composition across different screen sizes.

My role

  • Pixel Perfect front-end coding from graphic design
  • Full responsiveness – mobile, tablet, desktop, large screens
  • Typography refinement and layout proportions
  • Subtle animations and interaction effects
  • Image optimisation and loading performance
  • Semantic HTML and organised CSS architecture

Result

An elegant, lightweight and visually polished website that highlights the exclusive character of the brand and the quality of its products.

Delivered with great attention to detail and clean code, ensuring stability and readiness for further development.
