Client: FlowAir

FlowAir

Product page front-end – animations, parallax and interactive gallery.

Brief

The site was built as a dynamic presentation of the FlowAir SLIM air curtain product. The goal was a modern, engaging page that attractively communicates the device's functionality and real-world applications.

Multimedia elements – time-lapse videos, scroll animations and a parallax effect – were key to visually supporting the product communication. High performance and smooth operation across all devices were equally important.

My role

  • Pixel Perfect front-end coding from graphic design
  • Parallax effects and scroll-triggered animations
  • Time-lapse video integration showcasing the product in action
  • Custom interactive gallery script
  • Multimedia performance optimisation
  • Full responsiveness – mobile, tablet, desktop

Result

A modern product page that dynamically and attractively presents the FlowAir SLIM air curtain. Animations, parallax and video content help users better understand the product's functionality.

The project combines an accurate reproduction of the graphic design with an interactive front-end and optimised performance – working smoothly on both desktop and mobile.
